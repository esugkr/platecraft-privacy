PlateCraft Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026 • Android App: com.aistudio.platecraft.vkmqrx

1. Account & Authentication

PlateCraft uses Google Sign-In and Firebase Authentication to identify your account (user ID and email address). This keeps your saved gyms, plate conversion rules, and workout logs private to you and synced across your devices.

2. Gym Photos & AI Equipment Analysis

When you take a photo with the camera or choose images using the Android Photo Picker, those images are transmitted securely to Firebase AI Logic (Gemini) solely to detect gym equipment, barbell/plate units, and build your workout routine. PlateCraft does not request broad media library access and does not permanently store your gym photos on remote cloud storage.

3. Gym Memory, Preferences & Workout Logs

Your primary training goal, unit preferences (kg/lb), memorized gym profiles (equipment list, bar weights, plate sets, and stack steps), and completed workout logs are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and cached locally on your device for offline gym-floor access.

4. Data Security & Third-Party Sharing

All network traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Firestore security rules restrict reads and writes strictly to your authenticated user ID, and Firebase App Check protects backend services against unauthorized access. PlateCraft displays no third-party ads and never sells or shares your personal or fitness data with data brokers.

5. Retention, Deletion & Contact

You can delete any memorized gym profile or workout session directly within PlateCraft at any time, which removes it from both your offline cache and Cloud Firestore. To request complete deletion of your account record or ask privacy questions, contact ezra.baydur@gmail.com.
